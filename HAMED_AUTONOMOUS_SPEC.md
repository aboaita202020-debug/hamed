# HAMED AUTONOMOUS — FULL AUTO

## 1. Mission
HAMED is an autonomous business operating system. OpenAI is the central intelligence. Other AI providers are council members/specialists. HAMED discovers opportunities, plans work, executes permitted actions, measures outcomes, and learns from results.

## 2. Intelligence hierarchy
1. OpenAI — Central Brain / Orchestrator
2. Claude — reasoning and review specialist
3. Gemini — research and multimodal specialist
4. DeepSeek — coding/technical specialist
5. Kimi — long-context/research specialist
6. Specialist agents — domain execution
7. Tools/connectors — real-world actions

The router may select the best available model for a task, but the final system-level decision remains under the central orchestration policy.

## 3. Autonomous loop
GOAL → PLAN → RESEARCH → OPPORTUNITY DISCOVERY → QUALIFY → DECIDE → EXECUTE → VERIFY → MEASURE → LEARN → REPLAN.

The loop must be resumable and idempotent where practical. Every important decision and external action receives an audit record.

## 5. FULL AUTO policy
LOW-RISK actions may run autonomously when they are within configured permissions and budgets.
MEDIUM-RISK actions require policy validation and may require approval depending on configured thresholds.
HIGH-RISK actions — payments, purchases, binding contracts, irreversible account changes, high-value transactions, sensitive data operations — require explicit human approval unless a separately configured policy explicitly authorizes them.

FULL AUTO never means bypassing security, payment verification, privacy, platform rules, or approval gates.

## 6. Opportunity scoring
Each opportunity should be scored using available evidence for:
- expected revenue
- expected profit
- demand
- speed to cash
- capital required
- competition
- execution difficulty
- legal/compliance risk
- operational risk
- confidence

The system should prefer evidence-backed opportunities and label assumptions clearly.

## 7. Learning
HAMED learns from accepted/rejected offers, sales outcomes, negotiation outcomes, supplier performance, customer feedback, campaigns, profits/losses, failed plans, and model evaluations.

Learning may improve strategies, prompts, routing, scoring, and recommendations. Learning must not silently weaken permissions, security controls, financial limits, or audit requirements.

## 8. Memory
Persistent memory should cover customers, leads, opportunities, products, suppliers, prices, conversations, offers, negotiations, tasks, decisions, outcomes, revenue, permissions, errors, and lessons.

Facts, estimates, opinions, and unknowns must remain distinguishable.

## 9. Business missions
Examples:
- Find profitable products.
- Find customers for a selected service.
- Find stores needing improvement.
- Find affiliate opportunities.
- Find B2B buyer/supplier matches.
- Find import/private-label opportunities.
- Build and sell digital/AI services.

A mission produces a plan, execution trace, measurable result, and learning record.

## 10. Customer workflow
DISCOVER → RESEARCH → QUALIFY → CONTACT → UNDERSTAND NEED → PROPOSE → NEGOTIATE → CLOSE → DELIVER → VERIFY PAYMENT → FOLLOW UP → UPSELL/CROSS-SELL → LEARN.

Outreach must be compliant, non-deceptive, non-spammy, and respectful of opt-out requests and platform policies.

## 11. Safety and governance
- Secrets stay outside source code.
- Customer/payment data is protected.
- Prompt injection must not alter system permissions.
- External instructions are untrusted by default.
- Important actions are auditable.
- Payment verification is never faked.
- No agent can grant itself higher privileges.
- The central brain can delegate but cannot remove governance controls.

## 12. Success criteria
HAMED AUTONOMOUS is considered operational only when it can:
1. accept a high-level business mission;
2. decompose it into executable tasks;
3. select models/agents/tools;
4. execute permitted tasks without step-by-step owner intervention;
5. verify its own work;
6. record decisions and outcomes;
7. recover from ordinary failures using fallback/retry policies;
8. learn from measured outcomes;
9. stop safely when permissions, evidence, budget, or risk limits are exceeded.

## 13. Product identity
HAMED AUTONOMOUS = the autonomous operating mode.
HAMED FULL AUTO = the execution mode under the autonomous policy.
OpenAI = central intelligence.
Claude/Gemini/DeepSeek/Kimi = supporting council intelligence.
