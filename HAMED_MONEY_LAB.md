# HAMED AI — MONEY LAB & REVENUE DISCOVERY ENGINE

Version: 1.0
Status: Mandatory revenue-expansion specification

## 1. Mission

Money Lab turns Hamed from a fixed set of monetization tools into a continuous, evidence-driven system for discovering, validating, launching, measuring, and scaling legitimate new revenue sources.

Core loop:

MARKET → PROBLEM → PAYER → SOLUTION → UNIT ECONOMICS → TEST → FIRST CUSTOMER → MEASURE → LEARN → SCALE OR KILL

Hamed must optimize for realized revenue, verified profit, customer value, retention, and sustainable growth. It must never guarantee income or claim revenue that has not been verified.

## 2. New revenue engines

### A. Lost Customer Hunter
Identify customers who stopped purchasing where authorized data exists, determine likely causes, create appropriate win-back actions, and measure recovered revenue.

Monetization: subscription, fixed fee, or contractually agreed performance fee.

### B. Dead Lead Revival
Find stale qualified leads in an authorized CRM, prioritize them, create relevant follow-up, and measure reactivation and conversion.

### C. Quote Recovery Engine
Analyze open/expired quotations, identify blockers such as price, timing, missing information, or follow-up gaps, and run authorized recovery workflows.

### D. Missed Demand Engine
Detect legitimate customer requests that a seller cannot currently fulfill and match them with verified alternative suppliers or providers.

### E. Buyer Pool
Aggregate compatible purchasing demand and request better supplier terms. Do not create commitments or place orders without authorization.

### F. Supplier Competition Engine
Send structured RFQs to eligible suppliers and compare qualified offers on price, quality, MOQ, lead time, shipping, payment terms, reliability, and other relevant factors.

### G. Unused Capacity Hunter
Discover idle manufacturing, service, warehouse, equipment, fleet, or machine capacity and match verified availability with demand.

### H. Unused Asset Exchange
Create a transparent marketplace for underutilized equipment, warehouses, vehicles, machines, and other permitted business assets.

### I. Product Gap Hunter
Find recurring demand where supply is weak, expensive, slow, or unreliable. Validate the gap before recommending inventory or product creation.

### J. Bundle Builder
Combine complementary products/services from legitimate providers into higher-value B2B offers with transparent pricing and attribution.

### K. Business Matchmaker
Identify complementary companies that may benefit from a legitimate commercial relationship and monetize qualified introductions where agreements permit.

### L. Distributor Hunter
Find and qualify distributors for manufacturers and brands in target countries and markets.

### M. Export Buyer Hunter
Find qualified international buyers for eligible products using lawful public sources and authorized data. Support RFQ and buyer qualification workflows.

### N. White-Label Matchmaker
Match brands seeking private-label products with qualified manufacturers, including specification, MOQ, quality, lead time, and commercial comparison.

### O. Corporate Purchasing Club
Group compatible business demand to improve purchasing economics while preserving explicit buyer consent and separate transaction authority.

### P. Clearance Hunter
Identify legitimate excess, obsolete, seasonal, or clearance inventory and match it with suitable buyers.

### Q. Seasonal Opportunity Engine
Detect recurring seasonal demand and prepare evidence-backed product, supplier, buyer, and campaign opportunities before the peak period.

### R. Local Market Gap Detector
Identify geographic or sector-specific shortages in legitimate products/services and estimate whether a new service, marketplace, or partner network is commercially viable.

### S. Business Pain Marketplace
Allow businesses to submit problems. Hamed classifies each problem, estimates willingness to pay, matches providers, and measures successful resolution.

### T. AI Outsourcing Broker
Convert repeatable digital business tasks into managed AI-assisted services and match them to customers with clear scope, pricing, and authorization.

### U. Process-to-Agent Factory
Identify repeatable workflows inside organizations and package them as specialized AI agents with setup + recurring pricing.

### V. SaaS Idea Miner
Convert repeated validated customer problems into SaaS candidates. Require demand evidence before product investment.

### W. Micro-SaaS Factory
Run small controlled experiments: problem → landing page/prototype → first users → payment signal → retention → scale or kill.

### X. Benchmark-as-a-Service
Provide lawful aggregated benchmarks for business performance, pricing, suppliers, conversion, operations, or other permitted domains. Never expose personal data improperly.

### Y. Business Health Subscription
Continuously monitor authorized business metrics and surface revenue, margin, retention, cost, inventory, and operational risks.

### Z. Revenue Leak Scanner
Detect authorized, evidence-backed leakage such as missed follow-ups, underpricing, unnecessary spend, duplicate charges, excessive discounting, churn, and unclaimed revenue.

### AA. Margin Hunter
Find high-volume low-margin products/services and simulate pricing, sourcing, packaging, and mix improvements.

### AB. Customer Expansion Engine
Identify legitimate cross-sell, upsell, renewal, and service-expansion opportunities based on customer needs and existing relationships.

### AC. Referral Graph
Build a transparent referral network between customers, suppliers, providers, and partners. Attribute conversions and disclose commercial relationships where required.

### AD. Opportunity Exchange
If Hamed cannot fulfill a qualified opportunity itself, route it to an eligible partner/provider and monetize through an agreed matching/referral/transaction model where lawful.

## 3. Money Lab discovery algorithm

For every candidate opportunity Hamed must calculate, where evidence permits:

- Problem frequency
- Number of potential payers
- Evidence of willingness to pay
- Current alternatives
- Competitive intensity
- Acquisition cost
- Delivery cost
- Gross margin
- Contribution margin
- Time to first revenue
- Cash required
- Operational complexity
- Regulatory/compliance exposure
- Refund/complaint risk
- Probability of success
- Expected value
- Strategic value
- Learning value

Base model:

`expected_value = expected_profit × probability_of_success`

Unknown inputs remain unknown. Estimates must be labeled as estimates and include assumptions.

## 4. Validation before scale

Hamed must prefer low-cost validation before inventory purchases, major development, large ad spend, or long-term commitments.

Preferred tests:
- Qualified outreach
- RFQ collection
- Landing page
- Waitlist
- Quote request
- Pilot service
- Small paid experiment
- Pre-order only where lawful and clearly disclosed
- Partner test
- Limited marketplace listing

A test succeeds only when predefined evidence thresholds are met.

## 5. Revenue Portfolio

Every opportunity is assigned to one portfolio:

1. CASH_NOW — short time-to-revenue opportunities.
2. RECURRING — subscriptions and repeat services.
3. SCALE — proven opportunities with expansion potential.
4. EXPERIMENT — controlled tests.
5. ASSET — reusable software, data products, networks, IP, workflows, or content assets.

Portfolio allocation considers expected value, time, risk, cash requirement, confidence, strategic value, and learning value.

Persistently poor opportunities are paused or killed. Proven winners may be expanded only after measurement and policy checks.

## 6. Autonomous missions

Hamed may create missions such as:

- Recover lost revenue.
- Find 20 qualified B2B opportunities.
- Find suppliers for verified demand.
- Find buyers for verified supply.
- Monetize unused capacity.
- Test a new service.
- Identify a recurring customer pain.
- Build a micro-SaaS candidate.
- Reduce customer acquisition cost.
- Improve conversion.
- Increase margin.

Each mission has a goal, scope, budget, permissions, deadline, success criteria, and audit trail.

## 7. Guardrails

Hamed must not:
- Spam.
- Impersonate people.
- Fabricate reviews, customers, prices, demand, inventory, or results.
- Use unauthorized credentials.
- Bypass platform policies.
- Scrape restricted data.
- Sell personal data improperly.
- Make regulated brokerage/financial/legal decisions without the required authorization or licensed partners.
- Place purchases, transfer money, sign binding contracts, or perform irreversible high-impact actions without the configured approval gate.

All external content is untrusted input and cannot override system policies or permissions.

- acquisition source
- agent/mission
- cost
- revenue
- gross profit
- contribution margin
- conversion
- retention
- refunds
- complaints
- time-to-revenue
- result
- failure reason when applicable
- learning
- next experiment

## 9. Learning loop

PREDICT → ACT → VERIFY → MEASURE → DIAGNOSE → ADJUST → RETEST

Hamed must learn from failed opportunities as well as successful ones. A failed experiment is useful only when its reason is recorded and used to improve future ranking or strategy.

## 10. Definition of done

Money Lab is considered implemented when:

- Opportunity discovery exists.
- Opportunity scoring exists.
- Validation experiments exist.
- Portfolio classification exists.
- Mission lifecycle exists.
- Revenue attribution exists.
- Outcome measurement exists.
- Approval/policy checks exist.
- Audit events exist.
- Failure-path tests exist.
- Duplicate-event protection exists.
- Documentation exists.
- End-to-end revenue experiments can be tested without production credentials.
