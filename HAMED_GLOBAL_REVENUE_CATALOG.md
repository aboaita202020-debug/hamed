### Import & global sourcing — Hamed Import Engine

Hamed must support an import workflow for Egypt, Gulf, and other supported markets:

DEMAND DISCOVERY → PRODUCT VALIDATION → SUPPLIER HUNT → SUPPLIER EVIDENCE → RFQ → QUOTE COMPARISON → LANDED COST → COMPLIANCE CHECK → BUYER DISCOVERY → OFFER → NEGOTIATION → APPROVAL → PROCUREMENT → LOGISTICS → DELIVERY VERIFICATION → PROFIT MEASUREMENT

Candidate categories include:
- Industrial equipment and spare parts
- Packaging and packaging components
- Raw materials subject to applicable controls
- Restaurant/hotel equipment and supplies
- Workshop and maintenance tools
- Permitted electronics and accessories
- Energy-efficiency equipment
- Agriculture/irrigation supplies
- Storage and warehouse equipment
- Occupational safety equipment
- Household goods
- E-commerce supplies
- Automotive/equipment parts subject to applicable rules
- Bakery/food-service equipment
- Small-factory equipment
- Private-label products
- B2B products with local supply gaps
- Seasonal products
- Verified surplus/clearance inventory

Import economics must calculate, when data is available:
`landed_cost = supplier_cost + shipping + insurance + known_taxes + known_fees + clearance + inland_transport + storage + financing + expected_loss`

Never present unknown duties, taxes, permits, delivery dates, or supplier claims as facts. Regulated products, customs classification, import licensing, food/cosmetics/medical/safety requirements, and other controlled categories require appropriate local professional or licensed-partner review.

## 6. Permission boundaries

Autonomous: research, analysis, opportunity discovery, drafting, simulation, internal scoring, low-risk permitted workflows.

Approval-gated: purchases, payments, binding contracts, regulated brokerage/financial actions, account/security changes, irreversible publishing, material refunds, or other high-impact actions.

No system feature may bypass server-side authorization.

## 7. Definition of done for each catalog item

A catalog item is not "implemented" merely because it is listed. It is implemented only when code, configuration, permissions, tests, audit events, metrics/attribution, billing/commission logic where applicable, compliance checks, documentation, and an end-to-end test exist.

## 8. Success metrics

Track realized revenue, gross profit, contribution margin, CAC where measurable, retention, churn, refunds, complaints, customer satisfaction, affiliate earnings, marketplace GMV/take-rate, subscription MRR/ARR, cost to serve, and verified savings.

Never guarantee a specific income level. Optimize for verified economic outcomes.
