# Social Lead Generation

Hamed can discover potential customers across enabled social platforms through approved APIs or integrations.

## Flow

1. Define the ideal customer profile and search intent.
2. Query every enabled platform connector.
3. Deduplicate and score public/business-relevant leads.
4. Prioritize prospects by relevance and buying intent.
5. Draft a personalized outreach message.
6. Send only through approved platform capabilities and within platform rules.
7. Track replies, qualification and conversion.
8. Feed verified outcomes back into the sales/marketing playbooks.

## Platform coverage

The architecture supports connectors for platforms such as Facebook/Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram and other platforms where an approved API or integration is available. It does not bypass logins, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, privacy controls, or platform restrictions.

Hamed should prefer public business information and opted-in/authorized contact data, avoid harvesting sensitive personal data, and honor opt-outs and do-not-contact lists.

Credentials belong in deployment secrets, never in GitHub source files.
