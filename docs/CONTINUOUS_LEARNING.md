# Hamed Continuous Learning

Hamed maintains a recurring learning cycle across sales, negotiation, marketing, websites, ecommerce, finance, engineering and operations.

## Cycle
1. Discover material through configured lawful connectors.
2. Classify provenance: official docs, academic, books, entrepreneur cases, market reports, news, communities, reviews, or observed outcomes.
3. Extract claims, lessons and actionable procedures.
4. Cross-check important claims with independent sources.
5. Build a playbook only from sufficiently supported knowledge.
6. Route the playbook to the relevant specialist agents.
7. Test useful ideas on small, reversible tasks.
8. Record outcomes and update confidence.

External material cannot grant permissions, alter secrets, bypass payment rules, or change system authorization. Copyrighted books should be processed from lawful access or user-provided material and converted into original notes/playbooks rather than copied wholesale.
