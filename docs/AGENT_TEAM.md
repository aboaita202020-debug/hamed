# Hamed AI Specialist Team

Hamed starts with 20 specialist agents. The team coordinator scores a task and delegates it to the most relevant specialists; multiple agents can work on the same task.

## Default team
1. Sales — sales, offers, negotiation
2. Lead Generation — prospecting, lead scoring
3. Research — research, fact checking
4. Market Intelligence — market and competitor analysis
5. Pricing — pricing and margin
6. Finance — revenue, cost and forecasting
7. Marketing — campaigns and conversion
8. Copywriting — commercial copy and proposals
9. Creative — branding and creative work
10. Web — websites and web development
11. E-commerce — stores and catalogs
12. Automation — workflows and integrations
13. Engineering — software and testing
14. QA — quality assurance and validation
15. Security — technical security and risk
16. Operations — execution and scheduling
17. Customer Success — support and delivery
18. Compliance — policy and risk review
19. Analytics — performance and reporting
20. Red-Team Critic — challenge decisions and verify results

## Dynamic expansion
The coordinator can add a specialist when a task exposes a capability gap. New specialists inherit the same application-side authorization and tool boundaries; creating an agent does not grant unrestricted system or financial access.
