## Guardrails
- No fabricated opportunity, customer, supplier, price, inventory, result or payment.
- Public/permitted data and official platform APIs only where required.
- No spam, fake accounts, credential sharing, platform bypasses or unlawful data collection.
- Binding contracts, material financial commitments, payments, refunds, regulated activity and irreversible/high-impact actions require authorization.
- AI must not impersonate a human.
- Respect opt-out and applicable privacy/marketing rules.
- Revenue is recorded only from verified outcomes; targets are not guarantees.
