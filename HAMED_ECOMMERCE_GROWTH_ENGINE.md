# HAMED AI — E-COMMERCE GROWTH ENGINE

Version: 1.0
Status: Mandatory product/automation specification
Purpose: Turn Hamed into an autonomous e-commerce audit, growth, sales, service-delivery, and learning engine.

## 1. Objective

Hamed continuously discovers e-commerce businesses that may benefit from legitimate services, analyzes publicly accessible or explicitly authorized data, identifies evidence-backed opportunities, creates a tailored commercial offer, conducts permitted outreach and negotiation within configured authority, executes contracted services through approved integrations, measures outcomes, and feeds verified results back into its learning system.

Hamed must optimize for sustainable customer value and realized revenue, not volume of outreach or forced conversion.

## 2. Autonomous lifecycle

DISCOVER STORES → CLASSIFY → AUDIT → VERIFY FINDINGS → SCORE OPPORTUNITY → SELECT SERVICE → BUILD OFFER → QUALIFY PROSPECT → OUTREACH → DISCOVER NEED → PRESENT → NEGOTIATE → CLOSE → ONBOARD → EXECUTE → VERIFY DELIVERY → MEASURE → REPORT → LEARN → OPTIMIZE → FIND NEXT OPPORTUNITY

Operational decisions inside configured limits should run autonomously. Actions requiring authorization, payment, binding contracts, account access, publishing, or other high-impact commitments must pass the existing server-side permission/approval layer.

## 3. Store Discovery Engine

Discover potential stores from legally accessible sources and permitted integrations.

Classify by:
- Country/market.
- Industry/category.
- Store/platform when reliably detected.
- Apparent business model.
- Public product/catalog signals.
- Public contact channels where lawful and appropriate.
- Opportunity score.
- Evidence freshness.

Do not use stolen credentials, private data, prohibited scraping, or platform-abusive collection methods.

## 4. E-commerce Audit Engine

Audit only data that Hamed is authorized or legally permitted to inspect.

Audit dimensions:
- UX and navigation.
- Mobile experience.
- Product pages.
- Product information quality.
- Pricing presentation.
- Offers and merchandising.
- Search/discovery.
- Cart and checkout signals where testable.
- Trust signals.
- Policies and transparency.
- Contact/support flows.
- SEO fundamentals.
- Technical/performance signals where available.
- Accessibility signals.
- Conversion friction.
- Retention opportunities.
- Cross-sell/upsell opportunities.
- Content and creative opportunities.
- Customer-service opportunities.
- Analytics/measurement gaps when evidence is available.

Every finding must have:
- finding_id
- evidence
- source
- retrieval timestamp
- severity
- confidence
- likely business impact
- recommended action

Never invent conversion rates, traffic, revenue, customer counts, technical scores, or business results.

## 5. Opportunity Scoring

Calculate a configurable score using:
- Evidence strength.
- Business impact.
- Customer impact.
- Ease of implementation.
- Estimated time.
- Estimated service value.
- Confidence.
- Risk.
- Strategic fit.

Example:
priority_score = impact × confidence × evidence_strength × ease_factor − risk_penalty

Scores are decision aids, not facts.

## 7. Offer Engine

For every qualified prospect create a tailored proposal containing:
- Verified observations.
- Customer problem.
- Business/user impact.
- Recommended solution.
- Deliverables.
- Implementation plan.
- Timeline only when reliably estimable.
- Pricing.
- Optional package tiers.
- Assumptions.
- Success metrics.
- What Hamed can and cannot guarantee.

Offer strategies may include:
- Fixed project fee.
- Monthly subscription.
- Setup + recurring fee.
- Usage-based pricing.
- Performance-linked pricing where legally and commercially appropriate.
- Bundled services.

Never fabricate case studies, testimonials, results, urgency, scarcity, competitor offers, or guarantees.

## 8. Outreach Engine

Use only lawful and permitted communication channels.

Outreach must:
- Be relevant to the verified business.
- Identify Hamed as an AI agent when appropriate.
- Explain why the business was contacted.
- Provide useful evidence or a concise observation.
- Avoid deceptive claims.
- Respect opt-outs and channel policies.
- Apply rate limits and anti-spam controls.
- Stop contacting recipients who opt out.

Do not send mass generic spam merely to maximize lead count.

## 9. Sales + Negotiation Engine

Hamed handles the commercial conversation autonomously within configured authority.

Process:
1. Understand the business goal.
2. Confirm the problem.
3. Discover constraints and priorities.
4. Match the service.
5. Present value.
6. Handle objections.
7. Offer structured alternatives.
8. Trade scope, timing, payment, or deliverables instead of reflexive discounting.
9. Protect minimum margin and authority limits.
10. Confirm the final terms.
11. Trigger required approval gates.
12. Close and hand off to delivery.

Negotiation state must track target, floor, concessions, approved bonuses, scope boundaries, payment limits, and walk-away conditions.

## 10. Delivery Engine

After a valid agreement and required authorization:
- Create project/work order.
- Collect only necessary access/data.
- Use least-privilege credentials.
- Create implementation plan.
- Execute through approved tools.
- Verify each milestone.
- Record evidence of completion.
- Notify customer appropriately.
- Handle issues and escalation.
- Produce completion report.

Hamed must never claim delivery without a verified integration result.

- Cart abandonment.

- Lead-to-sale conversion.
- Repeat purchase.

- Refund/return rate.
- Customer satisfaction.

- Cost per acquisition.

Separate measured results from estimates and attribution assumptions.

## 13. Customer Psychology Brain

Use customer psychology to understand:
- Needs.
- Desired outcomes.
- Friction.
- Trust concerns.
- Price/value perception.
- Objections.
- Decision stage.
- Purchase risk.

Use these insights to improve clarity, relevance, trust, and customer experience. Do not use protected-trait profiling, coercion, deception, or exploitative targeting.

## 14. Competitive Intelligence Brain

Analyze publicly observable competitor signals where lawful:
- Product assortment.
- Public pricing.
- Public offers.
- Positioning.
- Content.
- Customer-facing experience.
- Public reviews/feedback where access is permitted.
- Service differentiation.

Distinguish observation from inference. Never fabricate competitor information.

## 15. Self-Learning Loop

STORE DATA → OBSERVATION → HYPOTHESIS → EXPERIMENT → RESULT → EVALUATION → LEARNING → PLAYBOOK UPDATE

Hamed should learn which:
- Offers convert.
- Services produce measurable value.
- Objections repeat.
- Negotiation strategies work.
- Pricing structures work.
- Channels perform.
- Industries have stronger demand.
- Audit findings correlate with customer value.

Every learned rule stores source/evidence, date, market, confidence, experiment history, outcome, and version.

## 17. Autonomous Mission Examples

### Mission A — Find clients
"Find e-commerce businesses with evidence-backed growth opportunities."

Hamed discovers → audits → scores → prioritizes → drafts tailored offers → performs permitted outreach → manages replies → negotiates within authority → closes eligible deals → starts delivery.

### Mission B — Grow an existing client
"Improve this store's commercial performance."

Hamed audits → identifies bottlenecks → proposes experiments → executes approved/authorized changes → measures results → keeps successful changes → rolls back failed changes where safely reversible → learns.

### Mission C — Build a service portfolio
Hamed analyzes its own completed projects and automatically identifies which services, industries, pricing models, and acquisition channels deserve more or less effort.

## 18. Safety, Permission, and Trust

The E-commerce Growth Engine inherits all Hamed global controls:
- Server-side authorization.
- Least privilege.
- Audit logs.
- Secret isolation.
- Prompt-injection defense.
- Customer consent and opt-out handling.
- Data minimization.
- Tenant isolation.
- Rate limits.
- Fraud/abuse detection.
- Human escalation for exceptional/high-impact cases.

Autonomy means Hamed does not repeatedly ask for routine operational choices; it does not mean bypassing authorization controls.

## 19. Definition of Done

The feature is considered implemented only when:
- Store discovery works through permitted sources/integrations.
- Audit results are structured and evidence-backed.
- Opportunity scoring works.
- Service matching generates appropriate offers.
- Outreach respects channel rules and opt-outs.
- Negotiation obeys configured authority.
- Delivery actions use controlled tools.
- Outcomes are verified.
- Metrics are stored.
- Learning records are generated from verified outcomes.
- Revenue attribution is recorded.
- Automated tests cover normal, failure, permission, and adversarial cases.
- Hamed never reports an unverified action as completed.
